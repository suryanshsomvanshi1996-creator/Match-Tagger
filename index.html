Match Tagger turns match footage into event data. Play your video, log every pass, shot, tackle and press with a key or a tap on the pitch, and the analysis builds as you go: heatmaps, pass networks, expected threat, player profiles and clips.

It's made for analysts building a portfolio, coaches reviewing their own team and scouts comparing players — anyone who wants Opta-style data from a match they can watch.

What you can do

Everything runs from one page, on a laptop or a phone.

Tag events

16 actions — pass, cross, carry, dribble, shot, tackle, interception, recovery, clearance, block, pressure, duels, fouls, saves — with exact pitch location, outcome, receiver and an automatic phase of play.

Analyse the match

Heatmaps, pass maps, expected threat (xT), pass networks, shot maps and the 18-zone grid, filtered by team, player, phase or time.

Profile players

Radar charts per 90 minutes with percentile ranks against the same position, a heatmap and a written summary. Compare any two players.

Watch player clips

Pick a player and an action — every pass, shot or tackle plays back to back from your video, ready for a report or a coach.

Team stats

Possession, field tilt, PPDA, counter-press regains, passes per possession, box entries and direct speed.

Export and import

CSV for your Match Analysis Toolkit in Excel, a full data CSV with coordinates, PNG charts — and import Toolkit workbooks.

How to use it

Four steps from video to analysis. Your video plays from your own device and is never uploaded.

  1. Set up the matchIn Matches & squads, click New match, type both squads as “8 Iniesta”, pick which way each team shoots, and tick the starters.
  2. Open your videoOn the Tag tab, click Choose video… and pick the match file. It stays on your computer or phone.
  3. Tag as you watchType the shirt number (or tap the player), press the action key (or tap the button), click where it happened on the pitch, then Enter for successful or Z for not.
  4. AnalyseOpen Analyse for charts, player profiles and stats. Click a player in the pass network or the stats table to jump to his profile.

Your matches

The backup is one .json file with everything: all matches, events, line-ups, formations and substitutions. Keep it in your OneDrive folder. Restoring adds missing matches and updates older copies; a newer copy already here is kept.

Matches are saved in this browser on this device. Use Export full data (CSV) to back them up or move them to another device, then Import it there.

Import takes a Match Tagger “full data” CSV, or your Match Analysis Toolkit workbook (.xlsx): teams and squads come from Match Info, events from the Event Log. Toolkit events are placed at the centre of their zone.

One player per line: shirt number, then name, e.g. 8 Iniesta. Use the same names as your Match Analysis Toolkit so exports paste straight in.

Line-ups, positions & substitutions

Pick each team's formation, then choose a player for every spot from the drop-downs. That sets the starting XI and each player's position in one go (no typing). Positions pick the template on player profiles; starters and substitutions give minutes played for per-90 stats. Changes save automatically.

Substitutions pick the team, who went off, who came on (bench players are listed first) and the video time
